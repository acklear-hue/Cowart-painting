Who's behind the brush

Locally owned, detail-obsessed.

Cowart Painting is a locally owned painting company based in Indian Trail, serving the wider Charlotte area. Dylan Cowart runs every job personally — from the first walkthrough to the final touch-up — with the same prep standards whether it's a single accent wall or a full commercial repaint.

That means real masking and drop-cloth protection, drywall patched and sanded smooth before a single coat goes on, and color that goes on even, edge to edge.

How a project runs

Three steps, no surprises.

01 — Prep

Protect & patch

02 — Apply

Prime & paint

Proper primer where it's needed, then color applied in even coats — cut lines sharp, coverage consistent wall to wall.

03 — Finish

Walkthrough & touch-up

We walk the space with you before we call it done, and handle any touch-ups on the spot.
